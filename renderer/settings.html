Connect your browser (one time only)
  1. In Chrome, type chrome://extensions in the address bar and press Enter.
  2. Turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the penguin's extension folder.

To get the folder: press the button below, then copy the address from the window that opens and paste it into Chrome's folder picker.

Warning 0 means the penguin kicks the tab as soon as you open it.

Sites are blocked only while a focus timer is running. Ctrl+Shift+T brings a kicked tab back, but unsaved text may be lost.
